3. I asked Claude to assess possible failure modes for both functions

5. I asked Claude to go back over both functions and strengthen the

6. Claude implemented a second round of validation on top of the
   original guard clauses in matvec_multiply.py:
     - reject non-sequence arguments (None, a bare number, a string,

7. Claude rewrote matvec_tests.py to cover the new guards: non-sequence
